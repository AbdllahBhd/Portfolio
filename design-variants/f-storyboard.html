MATMAK: evolving a PPF cutting platform

MATMAK was already live when I joined. The job is not a rewrite: it is to extend an established ASP.NET Core SaaS while protecting day-to-day stability for real customers.

Situation
Established product with customers, dated interfaces, growing data, no dedicated ops.
What I did
Extended REST APIs, Razor Pages and workflows for auth, subscriptions, invoicing, pricing and cutting; redesigned the customer and admin UI; integrated Stripe subscriptions and webhooks; redesigned the schema and tuned EF Core queries; took over servers, databases, AWS and releases.
Result
About 40% faster affected reads and writes, a modern responsive UI, and one accountable owner for infrastructure.

XSHIELD: an online store, end to end

A UAE client needed a store that actually takes money and keeps running, not just a theme install.

Provisioned Linux hosting, domain, DNS and Google Workspace email; built custom PHP pages and plugins, API integrations and import/export workflows; configured Stripe and Network International for UAE checkout; launched and supported it.
Result
Live store taking UAE payments, delivered by one engineer from setup to go-live.

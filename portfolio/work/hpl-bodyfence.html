The context

The vehicle patterns and car kits are served by a dedicated MATMAK API. The HEXIS Precut Lab pages run on WordPress, on the BODYFENCE website, with their own WordPress database. The plugin shows that catalogue on those pages by reading it from MATMAK's API.

What I built

  • A custom WordPress plugin, written in PHP, that consumes a dedicated MATMAK API.
  • Shortcodes that display the vehicle patterns and car kits wherever they are placed on a page.
